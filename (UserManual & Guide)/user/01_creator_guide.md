# Creator guide

This guide is for **student creators**.

Use it to learn, complete homework, and submit your contest video.

---

## Before you begin

You need:

- An approved creator account
- Access to your school team (if your school uses teams)
- The MyLENS website link

---

## 1. Home

After login, open **Home**.

Here you can usually see:

- A short event briefing
- Your next step (for example: open lessons, or create a submission)
- Learning progress
- Submission counts (drafts, waiting for review, approved)

### What to do

1. Read the next-step card
2. Follow the button it gives you
3. Come back to Home anytime to check progress

---

## 2. Learning

Open **Learning** in the menu.

You will see two tabs:

| Tab | What it is for |
|-----|----------------|
| **Lessons** | Watch classes, videos, documents, and links |
| **My tasks** | Submit homework for mentor review |

### Important

Homework is **not** inside Lessons.  
Use **My tasks** for assignments.

---

## 3. Lessons

1. Open **Learning → Lessons**
2. Use the course outline on the side (modules and lessons)
3. Click a lesson to open it
4. Watch, read, or join the class
5. When finished, use **Mark as complete** (for normal lessons)

### Lesson types you may see

| Type | What you do |
|------|-------------|
| Live | Join at the scheduled time |
| Video | Watch the recording |
| Document | Open the file |
| Link | Open the resource |

### Tips

- Look at the module name so you know which topic you are in
- Complete lessons in order when possible
- Live class links appear when the mentor publishes them

---

## 4. My tasks (homework)

1. Open **Learning → My tasks**
2. Choose a task from the list
3. Read the instructions carefully
4. Add your answer (text, link, and/or file — as required)
5. Submit for review

### Task status meanings

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Not started | You have not submitted yet |
| Draft | Saved, not sent |
| Submitted | Waiting for mentor |
| In review | Mentor is checking |
| Needs revision | Fix and submit again |
| Approved | Done — progress is recorded |
| Rejected | Not accepted (read mentor notes) |

### Good habits

- Check the due date
- Read mentor feedback before re-submitting
- Do not use Submissions for homework

---

## 5. Contest video submissions

Open **Submissions**.

This is for your **tourism video entry**, not class homework.

### Create a new submission

1. Open **Submissions**
2. Choose create / new submission
3. Fill in title, category, location, and details
4. Add your video link (or follow the form instructions)
5. Save as draft, or submit for review when ready

### Submission status meanings

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| Draft | Only you can edit freely |
| Submitted / under review | Waiting for admin decision |
| Needs edits | Fix using admin notes, then send again |
| Approved | Accepted |
| Rejected | Not accepted |

### Submission window

- Submissions may open and close on set dates
- If create is blocked, the window may be closed
- Check Home for date information

---

## 6. Team

Open **Team** to:

- See your school teammates
- Use invite details if your school shares a team token

Ask your teacher if you are unsure how to join the correct team.

---

## 7. Profile

Open **Profile** to:

- Check your name and details
- Update your profile information
- Keep your contact details correct

---

## Quick checklist for creators

- [ ] Account approved
- [ ] Completed current lessons
- [ ] Submitted My tasks homework
- [ ] Created contest video submission (when the window is open)
- [ ] Checked Home for the next step

---

## If you feel lost

Ask yourself:

1. Am I doing **homework**? → Learning → **My tasks**
2. Am I doing my **contest video**? → **Submissions**
3. Am I **studying**? → Learning → **Lessons**
