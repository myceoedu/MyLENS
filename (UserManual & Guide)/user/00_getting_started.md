# Getting started

Welcome to **MyLENS**.

This short guide helps you open the website, sign in, and know where to go.

---

## What is MyLENS?

MyLENS helps students and schools:

- Learn filmmaking skills
- Complete class assignments
- Submit a tourism video for the campaign
- Work as a school team

Students use the **creator** screens.  
Mentors and staff use the **admin** screens.

---

## Two things people mix up

Please remember this:

| Screen | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Submissions** | Your contest **video** entry |
| **Learning → My tasks** | Class **homework** for your mentor |

They are not the same place.

---

## How to open MyLENS

1. Open the MyLENS website link you were given
2. Use a modern browser (Chrome, Edge, or Safari)
3. Sign in, or create an account if you are new

---

## Create an account

1. Open **Register**
2. Enter your details carefully
3. Join or select your school when asked
4. Submit

### After you register

- Your account may show as **pending**
- An admin must approve you before full access
- If you cannot enter the creator home, wait for approval or ask your teacher / MyLENS admin

---

## Sign in

1. Open **Login**
2. Enter your email and password
3. Tap or click sign in

### Forgot password?

1. On the login page, open **Forgot password**
2. Enter your email
3. Open the reset email and set a new password
4. Sign in again

---

## After login — where do I go?

| If you are a… | You should see… |
|---------------|-----------------|
| **Creator** (student) | Creator menu: Home, Submissions, Team, Learning, Profile |
| **Admin** (staff) | Admin menu: Overview, Submissions, Inquiries, People, Schools, Learning |

If you see the wrong area, ask an admin to check your account role.

|------|------------|

---

## Tips for a smooth start

- Use the same email every time
- Check spelling of school name / invite details
- If a page looks empty, refresh once
- If learning content is missing, it may still be a **draft** (not published yet)

---

## Need help?

1. Read the Creator or Admin guide for your role
2. Check Troubleshooting
3. Contact your teacher or MyLENS admin
